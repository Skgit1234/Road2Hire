# Evaluation Metrics

## 1. Resume Skill Extraction
Measures how accurately the system identifies relevant skills from a candidate's resume.

- Precision — Measures how many extracted skills are correct.
- Recall — Measures how many relevant skills are successfully extracted.
- F1-score — Provides a combined measure of Precision and Recall.

## 2. Skill Matching
Measures how accurately candidate skills are matched with required job or career skills.

- Precision — Measures how many identified matches are relevant.
- Recall — Measures how many relevant skill matches are identified.
- F1-score — Provides a combined measure of Precision and Recall.

## 3. Skill-Gap Identification
Measures how accurately the system identifies skills that a candidate is missing for a target job or career.

- Precision — Measures how many identified skill gaps are actual gaps.
- Recall — Measures how many actual skill gaps are identified.
- F1-score — Provides a combined measure of Precision and Recall.

## 4. Career & Job Recommendation
Measures the accuracy and usefulness of recommended careers and jobs.

- Top-K Accuracy — Measures whether a relevant career or job appears among the top recommendations.
- Relevance — Measures how suitable the recommended careers or jobs are for the candidate.

## 5. Explainability
Measures whether the system provides understandable reasons for its skill-gap and career recommendations.

- Explanation Relevance — Measures whether explanations are relevant to the recommendation.
- User Rating — Measures how users rate the quality and understandability of explanations.

## 6. User Evaluation
Measures the overall user perception of the Road2Hire system.

- Relevance — Measures whether the recommendations are relevant to the user's career needs.
- Clarity — Measures how easily users understand the results and recommendations.
- Usefulness — Measures whether users find the system helpful for career and learning decisions.
