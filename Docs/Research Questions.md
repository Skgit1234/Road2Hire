# Research Questions

## RQ1 — Skill Gap Identification

**How effectively can NLP and semantic skill matching techniques identify the skill gaps between a candidate's resume and the requirements of a target career or job?**

## RQ2 — Career Recommendation

**How effectively can knowledge graphs and career prediction techniques use identified skill gaps to recommend suitable career paths and learning requirements?**

## RQ3 — Personalized & Explainable Guidance

**How effectively can explainable AI and personalized RAG provide understandable and personalized career and learning recommendations based on a candidate's skills and skill gaps?**
